Battambang Province, located in northwestern Cambodia, is known for its rich cultural heritage, peaceful atmosphere, and scenic countryside. Often called the “rice bowl” of the country, it is famous for its fertile land and traditional farming communities.

Visitors are drawn to its charming colonial architecture, ancient temples, and unique attractions like the bamboo train. With a blend of history, art, and natural beauty, Battambang offers a calm and authentic glimpse into Cambodian life.
